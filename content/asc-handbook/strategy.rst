ASC Strategy
============

"Strategy" refers to a general plan or vision to achieve our goals (and
what those goals are).  Strategy can sometimes have a negative
connotation by being too abstract (or at a level disconnected from
what we actually do on the ground), but there is a value in knowing
the issues of our team is currently facing and what we need to work on
together to handle them.

This page contains both current and past strategy statements, to show
the evolution.
