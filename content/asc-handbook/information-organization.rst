Google Docs
-----------

- Google Docs is our main platform for notes and documents (with Aalto
  workspace / Aalto accounts).  It's not great to have all our stuff
  in a foreign cloud, but it's better than Microsoft stuff and lets us
  work efficiently.
- This is our main place for documents/presentations that don't
  naturally go in git, but it isn't suitable for very secret stuff.
- You have to activate the Aalto Google account first via `this form
  <https://www.aalto.fi/en/services/google-drive-registration-and-closing-of-an-account>`__.
  Don't use your own personal Google account.
- We have the following "Shared drives" (which should be used for most
  things, instead of putting it in your own accounts):

  - AaltoSciComp: General team stuff, Triton, etc.
  - AaltoRSE: Things related to customer projects and RSE
    procedures/presentations/etc.
  - Data Agents: Collaboration with the Data Agent network, in
    particular group meetings

- Everyone in ASC has access to all of these, splitting is only for
  organizations and not for access control.
