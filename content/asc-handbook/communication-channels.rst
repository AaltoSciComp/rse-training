Communication channels
======================


Chat systems
~~~~~~~~~~~~

- Zulip chat, its specialty is organizing information.
- Chat is for short term topics.  If it's more than a few days old,
  consider it obsolete.  Prioritize your short term time to what is
  important.
- Zulip topics allow you to follow what is most interesting to you.
- Topics are very important, since it allows people to
  scan... topics... and see what to read.  Try to make topics
  descriptive enough so that someone will know when they need to
  click.
- Don't try to follow everything unless you want.  Follow a few of the
  important meta channels (e.g. scince-it, rse-internal).
- Look at recent topics and click on what is relevant to you.
- If unreads get too long (for example after vacation), mark
  everything as read as read.  Then you will see what stays active and
  you can scroll back of those topics to get caught up.
- Users can ask questions in the public channels in our chat.  It's
  good to answer but we don't promise any service level there.
- Try to avoid private messages unless about a personal matter - it's
  better to use good topics and allow everyone to learn from our
  questions.
