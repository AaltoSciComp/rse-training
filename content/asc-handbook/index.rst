ASC handbook
============

This section contains onboarding and "how the team works" for staff of
Aalto SciComp.  Compared to the main site scicomp.aalto.fi, this is for
staff but open in accordance with our values.

Like everything, this documents past practice but that does not mean the
future has to be the same.

You shouldn't be alone, your supervisor and team is here to help you.
When you are an academic, there's this implicit expectation that you do
almost everything yourself (and if it takes a while, at least you
learned something).  We also have a spirit of learning, but you
shouldn't waste time on known things, when you could learn from someone
else on the team faster and better.

This handbook is also long, and while you should browse and know the
basics here, a lot of the purpose is to have a link so that someone who
knows of the page can send you the link.
