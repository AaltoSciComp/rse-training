Team leader
===========

Summary
-------

The Team leader ensures projects go well and people are doing will
within a small group of people (5-10).  They don't necessarily guide
most projects directly, but should ensure that the team members have
all resources necessary to do the best work.  Compared to the RSE
supervisor, they have less responsibility for the details of finance
and HR.


Main challenges / pitfalls
--------------------------

* Managing people and projects is a fundamentally different job than
  doing technical work.

* The team supervisor will still be managing projects and people, but
  also need to coordinate among other groups.



Expectations / checklists
-------------------------

* Ensure the best team assembles for each project, that has all the
  necessary roles depending on the project (project planner, project
  manager, domain expert, worker, etc. :inote:`of course there can be
  many overlaps of these and not every project needs each role
  specifically defined.`)
* Know what projects are going on well enough to know if any of them
  needed help or were going off-track and need an intervention.
* Know what people are working on and ensure that the people are OK.
* Ensure new projects get started well (making sure the right people
  are in the planning and starting).
* Manage the long-term schedule (avoiding over-committing or
  committing to the wrong projects).  Manage the amount of outreach to
  keep the backlog reasonable.
* Manage the project reporting system: make sure that things are
  tracked well enough to generate the reports we need.  Keep the
  systems accurate (but not excessively so).
* Make the yearly RSE reports from the collected data.

Some things that aren't currently team leader's responsibility:

* Finance management.
* HR management.
