IT in Aalto
===========

IT Services ("ITS") is the main IT unit of Aalto University.  It
should be noted that we are *not* part of IT Services and we are not
"IT people".  We are "Technical Services".  IT Services has many
diverse units from operations to user support.  Of particular note are
teams called "IT Services for Research" and the "Cloud team" which
also does a lot of AI platforms.
